About me

From visualizing data to engineering it

At Purdue I studied Computer and Information Technology with a focus on AI and data science, and spent a lot of that time on data visualization, turning messy datasets into interactive D3.js charts and Tableau and Power BI dashboards. Somewhere along the way I realized I cared just as much about where the data comes from as how it looks.

Now I'm a Data Engineer at Terraboost Media, an in-store marketing company, where I work on database management and keeping data flowing cleanly across the organization. I'm also an Orr Fellow. Outside of work I build personal projects to sharpen my Python and SQL. The biggest one is Sports Hub, a self-hosted sports data pipeline and dashboard.
