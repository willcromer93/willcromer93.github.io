Final project · Group of 3

World Happiness Index: a decade of data

Each country's happiness score over ten years, alongside the factors behind it: GDP per capita, social support, generosity, freedom of choice, and perceived corruption.

D3.jsHTMLMulti-variable

Key insights

  • GDP per capita correlates strongly with happiness scores.
  • Freedom of choice stands out as a primary factor among the top-ranked nations.
  • Switzerland's corruption perception has a notable effect on its happiness score.
  • The Nordic countries (Finland, Norway, Sweden) lean heavily on social support.

Mini project · Group of 3

Oceanus Folk: tracing a genre's influence

Built in my senior data visualization lab. Using a fictional dataset about the Oceanus Folk music genre, we visualized how it rose, spread, and shaped other genres over time.

D3.jsHTMLNetwork / time-series

Key insights

  • Oceanus Folk's influence was intermittent: gradual growth, then an eventual decline.
  • Indie Folk was the genre it influenced most.
  • The artist Sailor Shift drove much of its increase in traction.
  • Its own biggest inspirations were Doom Metal and Dream Pop.

Mini project · Solo

College Fight Songs: counting the clichés

An individual Tableau project breaking down university fight songs by the tropes they use, comparing them by conference and by decade.

TableauMapsCategorical analysis

Key insights

  • The Big Ten has the most total tropes, even though Michigan's song has zero.
  • The Pac-12 averages 3.9 tropes per school.
  • Songs written in the 1910s packed in the most tropes overall.
  • Rutgers comes closest to the "perfect" fight song, with 8 of 9 tropes.
